# FixEstimate Pay — 60-second demo script

## 0–8 seconds — The problem

Show the home screen and category tiles.

Voiceover: “A home repair starts with uncertainty: Is it safe, what will it cost, and should I call someone?”

## 8–22 seconds — Fast intake

Choose Plumbing, take a photo, show the local compression message, enter a short leak description, answer the two follow-up questions, and optionally enter a ZIP code.

Voiceover: “FixEstimate Pay turns a description and optional photo into a structured repair intake. Images are compressed on-device, and only a three-digit ZIP prefix is used for regional pricing.”

## 22–32 seconds — Nimiq Pay

Tap Unlock report and show the Nimiq Pay transaction approval.

Voiceover: “One clearly disclosed NIM payment unlocks the report. The wallet keeps control of the transaction and the app never sees private keys.”

## 32–50 seconds — Useful result

Scroll through the cost range, safety guidance, recommended path, parts, materials, tools, repair steps, and contractor-ready scope.

Voiceover: “The result gives a cautious cost range, safety boundaries, a shopping list, guided steps when DIY is appropriate, and a ready-to-share contractor scope.”

## 50–60 seconds — Trust and repeat value

Show Share report, Print / Save PDF, feedback controls, and Recent reports. Briefly select Electrical or Gas to show the professional-only warning.

Voiceover: “Reports can be shared, saved as a PDF, rated after use, and reopened later. Electrical and gas concerns are always handed to a licensed professional.”

End card: `FixEstimate Pay · Built by Phōstēr Labs · Powered by Nimiq Pay`
