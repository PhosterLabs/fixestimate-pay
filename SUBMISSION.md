# Competition Submission Draft

## Short description

FixEstimate Pay helps homeowners understand an unfamiliar repair before calling a contractor. Describe the issue, optionally add a photo, and approve a small NIM payment to unlock a cautious AI-generated cost range, likely causes, immediate safety steps, contractor-ready scope, and questions to ask before approving work.

The Mini App is designed for homeowners who need clarity—not a guaranteed quote. Nimiq Pay is fundamental to the experience: the connected wallet provides user-approved identity, each report has a unique on-chain NIM payment reference, and the server verifies the confirmed transaction before generating the report. FixEstimate Pay never receives wallet private keys.

Unlike generic AI chat or invoice tools, FixEstimate Pay turns a specific real-world problem into a structured, repeatable action plan. Clear warnings route dangerous electrical, gas, structural, fire, mold, asbestos, and major water hazards to qualified professionals. Reports remain available locally for easy comparison and contractor conversations.

Built by Phōstēr Labs as a standalone, open-source Nimiq Pay companion experience.
