# Competition Submission Draft

## Short description

FixEstimate Pay helps homeowners understand an unfamiliar repair before calling a contractor. Choose from ten repair categories, answer a few focused questions, optionally add an on-device compressed photo, and approve a small NIM payment to unlock a cautious AI-generated cost range, likely causes, immediate safety steps, repair guidance when appropriate, and contractor-ready scope.

The Mini App is designed for homeowners who need clarity—not a guaranteed quote. Nimiq Pay is fundamental to the experience: the connected wallet provides user-approved identity, each report has a unique on-chain NIM payment reference, and the server verifies the confirmed transaction before generating the report. FixEstimate Pay never receives wallet private keys.

Unlike generic AI chat or invoice tools, FixEstimate Pay turns a specific real-world problem into a structured, repeatable action plan. Electrical and gas reports are always professional-only, while other hazards are screened conservatively. Reports remain available locally and can be shared or printed to PDF. Optional ratings from verified paid reports help improve usefulness without collecting free-form feedback.

Built by Phōstēr Labs as a standalone, open-source Nimiq Pay companion experience.
