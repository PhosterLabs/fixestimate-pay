# Privacy Notice

Effective: September 12, 2026

FixEstimate Pay connects to the wallet address the user approves through Nimiq Pay. The app cannot access wallet private keys or recovery phrases.

For a paid report, the service processes the submitted repair description, selected category and urgency, approved wallet address, public transaction hash, and—only with explicit consent—an optional repair photo. Text and an optional photo are sent to Google Gemini solely to generate the requested report. Photos are not included in browser report history. Payment records and minimal operational logs are retained to prevent payment reuse, investigate errors, and operate the service.

Recent completed reports may be saved locally on the user's device. Clearing the Mini App's site data removes this local history. Blockchain transactions are public and cannot be deleted by FixEstimate Pay.

FixEstimate Pay does not sell personal information. Do not upload faces, identification documents, addresses, financial documents, or other sensitive information. Email privacy@phosterlabs.com for privacy questions.
