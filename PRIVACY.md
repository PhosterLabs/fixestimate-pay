# Privacy Notice

Effective: September 12, 2026

FixEstimate Pay connects to the wallet address the user approves through Nimiq Pay. The app cannot access wallet private keys or recovery phrases.

For a paid report, the service processes the submitted repair description, optional follow-up answers, selected category and urgency, approved wallet address, public transaction hash, and—only with explicit consent—an optional repair photo. If a user enters a five-digit ZIP code, only its first three digits are sent as a rough regional pricing signal. Report text, the three-digit ZIP prefix, and an optional photo are sent to Google Gemini solely to generate the requested report. Photos are compressed on the user's device and are not included in browser report history. Payment records and minimal operational logs are retained to prevent payment reuse, investigate errors, and operate the service.

Users may voluntarily rate a completed paid report. The selected helpfulness, price-range, and resolution feedback is stored with the report's request ID and public transaction hash so that feedback can be limited to verified reports. The service does not collect free-form feedback text.

Recent completed reports may be saved locally on the user's device. Clearing the Mini App's site data removes this local history. Blockchain transactions are public and cannot be deleted by FixEstimate Pay.

FixEstimate Pay does not sell personal information. Do not upload faces, identification documents, addresses, financial documents, or other sensitive information. Email privacy@phosterlabs.com for privacy questions.
