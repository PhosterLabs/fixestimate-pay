Privacy Notice

Effective September 12, 2026

Wallet information

FixEstimate Pay connects only to the wallet address you approve through Nimiq Pay. It cannot access your private keys or recovery phrase. Blockchain transactions are public and cannot be deleted by FixEstimate Pay.

Repair reports

To create a paid report, we process your repair description, category, urgency, approved wallet address, public transaction hash, and—only with explicit consent—an optional repair photo. Text and an optional photo are sent to Google Gemini solely to generate your requested report. Photos are not included in browser report history.

Storage

Minimal payment records and operational logs are retained to prevent payment reuse, investigate errors, and operate the service. Recent reports may be saved locally on your device. Clearing this Mini App's site data removes that local history.

Your choices

Do not upload faces, identification, addresses, financial documents, or other sensitive information. FixEstimate Pay does not sell personal information. Contact privacy@phosterlabs.com with privacy questions.
